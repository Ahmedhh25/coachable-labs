01 / About Us

What Is Coachable Labs?

Three ways to work with us. The main one is a six-session 1:1 engagement across a set arc, taking purpose, health, money and relationships together rather than one at a time. There are four-session engagements for couples and families. And there is a separate advisory track for founders and leaders. All of it is private-pay, and all of it starts with one conversation.

We built it for a particular person. Someone still delivering and running on fumes, whose résumé reads clean and whose household runs like a well-managed company, and who has started to wonder whether these were even the right targets. If that lands, keep reading. If it doesn't, this probably isn't for you.

02 / The Mechanism

You're Not Under-Informed. You're Over-Consumed and Under-Transformed.

You've read the books. You've probably done therapy. The advice was good and it arrived one domain at a time, from people who only ever saw their own slice. Nobody has put your work, the people closest to you, and who you take yourself to be in the same room. That is the thing this is built to do: one coach, one view of the whole situation, six sessions to work it through.

The framework is built on the research into what income and achievement do to day-to-day wellbeing at this level. Killingsworth's 2021 study found that wellbeing keeps climbing with income, and that a person's felt sense of control over their own life tracks closely with it.1

I've optimized my way into a life that works and doesn't feel like mine.

03 / What Changes

What You Get Back

At this point you are not short on talent or information. What's missing is structure, someone holding the standard, and one room where the whole picture is on the table.

Decisions get cheaper

Settle the purpose and most decisions stop being decisions. Most of the calendar relief comes from there.

Someone holds the standard

Accountability changes what you'll do when no one is watching. It is the best supported finding in the coaching literature and the least interesting to talk about.

Drift costs more than stress does

Stress you already know how to manage. Drift you don't. It compounds quietly and shows up years later as the sense that none of this is yours.

The wins read as yours

A result you can trace to your own decision can be repeated. One you can't trace never quite lands, whatever the size of it.

One room where you're honest

A full network and nobody to be straight with is standard at this level. This is the one room that isn't.

Systems that outlast the engagement

The arc has an end date. What you keep is the set of internal systems it puts in place, which is the reason it is finite.

04 / Before You Ask

If You Just Rolled Your Eyes at the Word "Coaching," So Did We

The skepticism is the right instinct. Most of what carries this label is encouragement you could get for free. Five questions worth answering before you go any further.

Fair question, and a lot of us had the same reaction. There are no affirmations here and no generic motivation. There is a framework, built on published research into what predicts wellbeing at this income level, run by someone who takes the complexity of your situation seriously.

This is built for people with no slack in the calendar. The arc is visible, it has phases, and it ends. If "no time" is the honest answer, that is usually worth one conversation on its own.

You're not under-informed. You're over-consumed and under-transformed. Insight has come to you one domain at a time, and nobody has ever put your work, your relationships, and your identity together in one place. That is the gap.

The confidentiality here is structural rather than a promise: private-pay, no insurance, no documentation trail. There is no file with your name on it, which is what makes saying the whole thing possible.

We don't discount. The price reflects the depth of the work and there is no padding in it. Most people here already spend comparable money on a mastermind membership or an executive program, so the open question is not whether to spend at this level. It is whether this is the right place to.

05 / How It Works

Four Steps, First Form to First Session

No pitch at any point in it.

  1. You request a conversation

    The form at the bottom of this page asks what is going on. Write it properly. We read it before we speak, so the call doesn't get spent on background.

  2. A thirty-minute call

    We work out where you are trying to get to and whether this is the right instrument for it. If it isn't, we say so on that call and point you somewhere better.

  3. We scope the work and quote a fee

    Once we both know what the work is. There is no tier menu to choose from and no package to compare, because the scope isn't the same for everyone.

  4. The engagement runs

    Six sessions for the 1:1 track, four for couples, across a set arc with phases and an end date. There is work between sessions. Everything is private-pay.

Before You Ask

If You Just Rolled Your Eyes at "Coaching," Good — So Did We

The skepticism is the correct instinct. Most of what carries this label is encouragement with a price tag. Here are the five questions worth having answered first.

"Is this just life coaching?"

Fair question — a lot of us have the same reaction. This isn't affirmations or generic motivation. It's a framework, built on published research into what actually predicts wellbeing at this income level, delivered by someone who takes your complexity seriously.

"I don't have time for this."

This is built for people with no slack in the calendar. There's a visible arc with phases — not open-ended sessions that drift. And if "no time" is the honest answer, that itself is usually worth one conversation.

"I've read the books, done therapy — what's different here?"

You're probably not under-informed. You're over-consumed and under-transformed — you've been handed insight one domain at a time, and no one's integrated your work, your relationships, and your identity together. That's the actual gap.

"Can I trust a stranger with things I can't say to anyone?"

The confidentiality architecture is structural, not a promise: private-pay, no insurance, no documentation trail. Full honesty is safe here by design.

"Is this worth the price?"

We don't discount, because the price reflects the depth of the work, not padding. Most clients here are already spending comparably on a mastermind membership or an executive coaching program — the question isn't whether to invest at this level, it's whether this is the right fit.

Every Engagement Starts With One Real Conversation

You write a short form when you request the call. We read it before we speak, so the conversation is about where you're actually trying to get to and whether this is the right instrument for it. No pitch. If it isn't a fit, we'll say so on that call.

Three ways in. There's no tier menu to compare — scope and fee are set after the first conversation, once we both know what the work actually is.

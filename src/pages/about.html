Who you'd be working with

Between us we have spent more than thirty-five years doing community work with young people across Raleigh, Durham and Chapel Hill.

Thousands of those conversations turned out to be the same handful of conversations. Someone capable, doing well by every visible measure, with one part of their life quietly paying for another and nobody in a position to say so. We built something structured because the ad-hoc version of that help ran out long before the need did.

What that structure is, and what it rests on, is set out on the approach page.

Is this you

  • You are still delivering, and you can no longer say what for.
  • You have read the books and done the work, and nothing has moved.
  • The calendar is full and none of it is yours.
  • You hit the number and it didn't land the way it was supposed to.
  • You have a full network and nobody you can be straight with.
  • You have outgrown starting over, and you want something that compounds.

What we work on

  • A purpose specific enough that it makes your decisions cheaper.
  • Habits that survive a calendar with no slack in it.
  • Money decisions that serve the life rather than the scoreboard.
  • What you are building, and who it is for.

What you get back

  • Decisions get cheaper. Settle what it is for and most decisions stop being decisions. Most of the calendar relief comes from there.
  • Someone holds the standard. Accountability changes what you do when nobody is watching. It is the best-supported finding in the coaching literature and the least interesting to talk about.
  • The wins read as yours. A result you can trace to your own decision can be repeated. One you can't trace never quite lands, whatever the size of it.

What the engagement is

  • Six one-to-one sessions, private, across a set arc with a stated end date.
  • A fuller assessment before the first session, so session one is not spent on background.
  • Work between sessions. The feedback is direct.
  • It ends. We are not trying to keep you — the point is that you leave with the systems.

Questions people actually ask

If you just rolled your eyes at the word coaching, so did we. The skepticism is the right instinct. Most of what carries this label is encouragement you could get for free.

What there is: an assessment across five areas of your life that your coach reads before the first session, a set arc rather than a topic checklist, work between sessions, direct feedback, and a finish rather than a standing appointment. Why we work across everything at once.

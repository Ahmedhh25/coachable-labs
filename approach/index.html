Why we work across everything at once

This is the one page where the argument gets made. It is not repeated anywhere else on the site.

The short version: the parts of your life are not separate problems, so we do not work them as separate problems.

Advice has reached you one domain at a time

You are not under-informed. The advice you have had was mostly good, and it arrived from people who each saw one slice of your situation: the career coach who never asked about the marriage, the financial adviser who never asked what the money was for, the friend who only ever sees you on a good day.

Nobody has put your work, the people closest to you, and who you take yourself to be in the same room. That is the gap this is built for: one coach, one view of the whole situation, and a set arc to work it through.

Book a 30-minute call

Half an hour on what is going on and where you are trying to get to, so we can both work out whether this is the right instrument for it. If it isn't, we will say so on the call and point you somewhere better.

What happens on it

  • You talk, mostly. We ask about the situation rather than about your goals.
  • We take one real thing you are dealing with and work it through with the framework, so you can see how the sessions actually run.
  • If it is a fit, we scope the work and agree a fee afterwards. Nothing is charged before or during this call.

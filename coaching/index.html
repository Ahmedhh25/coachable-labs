Five ways to work with us

All five are private and one-to-one. Each runs a set arc rather than an open-ended arrangement, with real work between the sessions. How long it runs depends on what you are dealing with, and we agree that together after the first conversation.

What each one is

Individual

The flagship. Purpose, health, money and relationships worked as one situation rather than four.

For someone who is functioning and can no longer say what any of it is for.

Purpose Habits Direction

Marriage

Pre-marital and marital work on expectations, money, family and communication. Individual sessions and joint ones.

For couples before the wedding, and for couples years past it.

Expectations Money Communication

Family

Boundaries with parents and in-laws, years of things left unsaid, and parenting through a stage nobody prepared you for.

For a family carrying tension that nobody in it starts the conversation about.

Boundaries Parenting Candor

Career

Choosing a major or a first direction, leaving a role cleanly, and staying relevant as AI resets the work in your field.

For students choosing a direction and for people who have outgrown one.

Direction Relevance Exits

Leaders

One-to-one work with the person leading it: direction, the decisions only they can make, and how they show up.

For a single founder or leader. Whole-organisation work goes to our sister company.

Choosing a direction

Changing one

The problem, in the words people use for it.

Everyone had an opinion about your career. Nobody asked what it was for.

That is the work. Not options ranked by salary, but the question underneath them, answered first so the options can be judged against something.

  • What the career is for, specific enough to rule options in and out.
  • The exit, if there is one: the case for it, the timing, and what it costs.
  • Relevance as the work changes, and what you would have to become to keep it.
  • The money side of the decision, separated from the identity side of it.

What the engagement is

  • One-to-one sessions across a set arc, agreed with you at the start.
  • A fuller assessment before the first session, so session one is not spent on background.
  • Work between sessions, including the parts of the decision you have been avoiding.
  • Private-pay, and it ends.
